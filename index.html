I'm a senior engineering leader specializing in building and scaling Technical Support Engineering organizations from the ground up. Leadership Philosophy

How I build and lead world-class technical support organizations

🌉 T3 as a Strategic Bridge

Technical Support Engineering should be a strategic bridge between Customer Experience and Engineering — not a ticket queue. I build organizations that reduce engineering interruptions, improve platform reliability, and drive product evolution from support signals.

📈 Measure Everything That Matters

Resolution quality, speed, customer experience, engineering escalation reduction, systemic prevention — I define success metrics that align with business outcomes and hold teams accountable to continuous improvement.

🧠 Hire for Potential, Develop for Excellence

I build career pathways that attract and retain top talent. Clear leveling frameworks, engineering-adjacent career tracks (Staff → Principal → Solutions Eng), and a culture of deep technical expertise combined with customer empathy.

⚡ Bias for Action

I maintain urgency without sacrificing quality. Whether it's a Sev-1 war room, an org design decision, or an automation initiative — I move quickly, make high-impact decisions independently, and course-correct with data.

🔄 Prevent, Don't Just Resolve

Every incident is a signal. I ensure post-incident reviews drive systemic fixes, not just patches. My teams translate patterns into product improvements, automation opportunities, and proactive monitoring — reducing future customer impact.

🤝 High EQ, Versatile Communication

I navigate complex stakeholder relationships — from coaching junior engineers through difficult customer conversations to influencing VP-level investment decisions. Empathy, clarity, and resilience are non-negotiable leadership traits.
